SALES INTELLIGENCE - BUSINESS RULES

Purpose
This knowledge base supports sales analysis and executive reporting.

Data sources
Snowflake is the authoritative source for exact sales figures and numerical aggregations.
Qdrant stores business definitions, reporting rules, and contextual documents.

Sales metric definitions
Revenue is the recorded sales revenue. It must not be described as profit because cost and profit data are not available in the current dataset.
Units sold is the quantity recorded in the units_sold field.
Base price is the original price before discount.
Discount percent is the discount percentage recorded for an order.
Final price is the price after discount.
Average selling price is the average selling price metric calculated by the approved Snowflake reporting view.
Unique orders counts distinct order identifiers.

Dataset fields
order_id: unique order identifier.
order_date: date of the order.
state: Indian state or region associated with the order.
zone: sales zone.
category: product category.
brand_type: brand positioning category recorded in the dataset.
customer_gender: recorded customer gender.
customer_age: recorded customer age.
base_price: price before discount.
discount_percent: recorded discount percentage.
final_price: price after discount.
units_sold: units sold.
revenue: recorded sales revenue.
sales_event: sales event classification.
competition_intensity: recorded competition intensity.
inventory_pressure: recorded inventory pressure.

Reporting rules
Use Snowflake SQL for exact counts, sums, averages, rankings, and date-based calculations.
Use retrieved business documents for definitions and business context.
Do not invent missing metrics, causes, forecasts, or business explanations.
Do not describe revenue as profit or infer margins without cost data.
Do not claim that a trend exists unless it has been verified using appropriately grouped data over time.
If evidence is insufficient, clearly state that the available data does not establish the answer.
When possible, identify the source document used for contextual answers.
