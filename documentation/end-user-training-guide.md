# End-User Training Guide
## Sales Intelligence RAG Platform

### 1. Introduction

This guide explains how business users can explore sales performance using the Sales Intelligence RAG Platform.

The platform combines:
- **Power BI:** For interactive sales reporting and visualization.
- **Snowflake:** For storing and querying structured sales data.
- **RAG Assistant:** For asking questions about business documentation and retrieving relevant information.

### 2. Understanding the Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard is intended to help users understand sales performance across different dimensions.

Key metrics available from the executive reporting layer include:

| KPI | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Total recorded sales revenue |
| Total Units Sold | Total quantity of products sold |
| Total Orders | Number of unique orders |
| Average Selling Price | Average value of the final selling price |
| Average Discount | Average discount percentage |
| Sales Period | First and last dates in the dataset |

### 3. How to Interpret Sales Performance

Users can explore sales performance by considering:

**Revenue trends**
- Compare revenue across months and years.
- Identify periods with higher or lower sales.
- Review month-over-month changes.

**Category performance**
- Compare revenue generated by product categories.
- Identify categories with higher sales volume.
- Review average discounts by category.

**Regional performance**
- Compare sales across states and zones.
- Identify regions contributing more revenue.
- Review units sold and average selling prices.

**Sales event performance**
- Compare revenue across sales events.
- Examine discount levels and units sold.

*Dashboard pages, filters and visuals should be documented here after verifying the final Power BI report.*

### 4. Using the RAG Assistant

The RAG assistant helps users find information from indexed business documentation.

Users can ask questions in natural language rather than searching through documents manually.

Example questions:

- What are the key sales reporting rules?
- How is total revenue calculated?
- Which sales KPIs are available?
- What is the difference between revenue and profit?
- Which dataset columns are used for regional analysis?

### 5. How RAG Works

The document question-answering process follows these steps:

1. Business documents are submitted for ingestion.
2. Documents are split into smaller text chunks.
3. An embedding model converts the chunks into numerical representations.
4. The embeddings are stored in Qdrant.
5. A user submits a question.
6. The system retrieves relevant document chunks.
7. The language model uses the retrieved context to generate an answer.

The RAG assistant is designed for contextual business-document questions. Structured numerical sales analysis should use Snowflake reporting data rather than relying on document retrieval.

### 6. Tips for Asking Better Questions

For more useful answers:

- Ask one clear question at a time.
- Mention the relevant metric or business topic.
- Specify a date range when asking about time-based information.
- Use the correct business terminology.
- Check the source information when validating important results.

Example:

Instead of asking:

"Tell me about sales."

Ask:

"How is monthly revenue growth calculated in this project?"

### 7. Important Limitations

- The dataset covers January 2023 to December 2025.
- Revenue does not represent profit.
- Profit margins cannot be calculated without product cost information.
- The RAG assistant may not have access to every business document.
- Answers should be checked against authoritative reporting data when numerical accuracy is important.
- Dashboard functionality depends on the visuals and filters actually implemented in the Power BI report.

### 8. Troubleshooting

| Issue | Recommended action |
|---|---|
| Dashboard does not load | Check the Power BI connection and data source |
| Sales figures look incorrect | Verify the reporting view in Snowflake |
| RAG assistant gives an irrelevant answer | Rephrase the question more clearly |
| RAG assistant cannot find information | Check whether the relevant document was ingested |
| Data appears outdated | Verify the latest data refresh and ingestion status |

### 9. Conclusion

The Sales Intelligence RAG Platform provides users with structured sales reporting and contextual document search.

Power BI supports visual analysis, Snowflake provides the analytical data foundation, and the RAG assistant helps users retrieve information from business documentation.
